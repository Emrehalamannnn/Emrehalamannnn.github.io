Wordlands support

Questions, a word that's wrong, a name that upsets you, anything at all: write to emrehlmn@icloud.com.

We read every email and usually answer within a few days. Wordlands has no account, so tell us what you see on the screen, which puzzle language you play in and, for a league, its name.

Questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Me > Settings and tap Restore purchases (it is also in Me > Shop). Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Plus shared through Family Sharing comes back the same way.

How do I cancel Plus?

Open Me > Settings > Manage Plus subscription, or the iPhone's Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Wordlands. Apple handles every subscription: cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period to stop the next payment. Plus stays on until then. Deleting the app or your data does not cancel it.

For a refund, ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com; we can't see or refund App Store payments.

How do I delete my synced data, or all my data?
  • Me > Settings > Synced leagues > Delete my synced data deletes your name, leagues and scores from the Wordlands server. Your leagues stay on your iPhone as link leagues.
  • Me > Settings > Delete all my data deletes your synced data and everything the app saved on your iPhone. Purchases stay with your Apple Account.
  • No longer have the app? Email us a score link you shared, or your display name and a league name, and we will delete your synced data.

Details are in the privacy policy.

How do leagues work?

A league is a small table for family and friends. Weeks run Monday to Sunday and your best 6 days count, with every language on the same 0-300 scale. Only the daily three (Scramble, Ladder, Hunch) count.

  • Synced leagues (13 and older, if you say yes once) fill up by themselves: scores go through our server in the EU. Join with a code such as K7P2QX-8M4D in Leagues > Join with a code, or with an invite link. Up to 30 people.
  • Link leagues need no server: you send your score as a link in any chat, and the others tap it or copy the message and tap Paste in Leagues.

There are no public leaderboards, no chat and no strangers. In a league you can hide a person or report a name; the person who made the league can remove members and make a new code.

What are the links people send me?

A score link looks like emrehalamannnn.github.io/r#… and an invite like emrehalamannnn.github.io/j#…. With Wordlands installed, tapping one opens the app. Otherwise the page shows the score or the invite and how to get the app. The data after "#" is read on your own phone and never sent to this website.

If tapping doesn't open the app, copy the whole message, open Wordlands > Leagues and tap Paste. A link that was cut or changed shows "This link looks broken or edited": ask for it again.

How do I report a word?

Tap the word (in the day's summary, after Hunch, or in your Word book) and choose Report this word. Reports stay on your iPhone until you send them: Me > Settings > Email my word reports opens your mail app with them, and nothing is sent until you press Send.

Who sees ads?

Players 13 and older without Plus or "No ads". Ads are never personalised, never appear in the middle of a puzzle, and players under 13 never see ads or offers. "No ads" removes every ad and gives you the ad rewards without watching.
