Wordlands privacy policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Wordlands: Daily Word Puzzles (the app for iPhone, and for Android once it is released) and this website, emrehalamannnn.github.io.

Who is responsible. The data controller is Emre Nezih Halaman, the maker of Wordlands. Contact: emrehlmn@icloud.com. "We" and "us" in this policy means him.

The short version

  • No account and no login. We never ask for your email address, phone number or real name.
  • Most of your data stays on your phone. Puzzles, streaks, your Word book, link leagues and settings are saved on your phone, and we do not receive them.
  • Synced leagues are optional. If you are 13 or older and say yes, our server in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany) keeps a random id, your display name, your age band, your leagues and your daily scores, so the people in your leagues see each other's scores. Under-13s never sync.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob and are never personalised. Google's ad software still collects some data to show, count and protect ads (section 4). Players under 13 see no ads, and Google's ad software is never started for them.
  • No tracking and no selling. The app never asks to track you and never reads your phone's advertising identifier. We do not sell your data.
  • You can delete it. Me > Settings > Synced leagues > Delete my synced data deletes what our server keeps. Me > Settings > Delete all my data deletes that and what the app saved on your phone.

The app saves these things in its own private storage on your phone. They are not sent to us unless you use synced leagues (section 2).

The age question. We ask your age group so that ads and synced leagues follow the rules for your age. On the phone it stays there. If you use synced leagues, our server keeps your age band (13-15, 16-17 or adult), never your exact age. The app does not send your age group to Google: for players under 18 it only tells Google's ad software to use the stricter rules for young people.

Backups. If you back up your phone (for example to iCloud or Google), this data can be part of that backup, under Apple's or Google's privacy policy.

2. Synced leagues (our server)

Synced leagues update by themselves: your scores reach the people in your leagues without pasting links. They are optional.

Who. Only players who say they are 13 or older, and only after they agree on a one-time screen ("Turn on synced leagues") that names the data, where it is kept and how to delete it. "Not now" keeps link leagues, which send nothing to us. Under-13s never sync: nothing about them is ever created on our server. If a player changes their age to under 13, the app deletes their synced data.

What our server keeps.

WhatDetails
A random id and a secretMade for synced leagues only (not your phone's id, not the advertising identifier). The secret stays in your phone's secure storage (the Keychain or the Android Keystore); the server keeps only a one-way hash of it, to check that requests come from your phone

Who sees it. Only the members of your leagues see your name and scores (up to 30 people who joined with the league's code or invite link). There are no public lists, no profiles and no chat. We look at name reports to keep leagues friendly.

Group stats. For adults, the app can show lines such as "61% of Español players solved today's Hunch" and, if you turn on "Show my place among players", how your day went. These are counts made from everyone's synced scores, shown only when 50 or more players played that language and day. They never contain names.

Your network address. Our server does not store IP addresses. To stop abuse (for example someone making many records), it counts requests per network address using a one-way keyed hash of the address that changes every hour and is deleted when its hour ends; for new records, a short 20-bit part of a hash (shared by thousands of addresses) is kept until the end of the day.

Where. On Supabase, our hosting provider, in its data centre in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Supabase processes the data for us under its data processing terms and keeps short technical logs of requests for security and operation.

How long. Daily scores are deleted after 120 days and name reports after 180 days. Your record, your name and your leagues are kept until you delete them. When you delete your synced data, leagues you created pass to the member who joined earliest, or are deleted when nobody is left.

What the app never sends for synced leagues: your email, contacts, location, exact age, advertising identifier or anything for ads. The synced data is used only to run leagues and the group stats, and is never shared with Google or anyone else.

3. What is inside a link you share

Link leagues work without our server: scores travel inside links that you send to people yourself, for example in WhatsApp or Messages.

  • A score link (https://emrehalamannnn.github.io/r#...) carries your player id, your display name, the puzzle language and day, your three scores and Hunch tries, the id of your league, the time you finished (only if "Show my finish time in shared scores" is on) and a short check code. The check code catches links that were cut or changed by mistake; it is not a password and not encryption.
  • An invite link (https://emrehalamannnn.github.io/j#...) carries the league's id and name, your player id and display name, and for a synced league its join code.
  • A shared text or weekly recap can also carry your streak and the names and week scores of a league's top three players.

Anyone who has a link can read what is in it and could forward it, so do not put anything in a name that you would not want others to see. The data sits after the "#" sign: when someone opens the link in a browser, the browser does not send that part to the website; the page reads it on their own device. When another player's app opens your link, your score is saved on their phone. The rules of the chat app you use apply to the message itself.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

Wordlands is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob to some players: a banner on some screens, at most one full-screen ad a day after the daily puzzles, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example for a hint).

Who sees ads. Players aged 13 and older who do not have Plus or "No ads". Google's ad software never starts for players under 13, with Plus, with "No ads" or in demo mode, and only starts after the first finished puzzle (or when you choose a rewarded ad).

Never personalised. Every ad request asks Google for non-personalised ads, for every player, at every age and in every country. Google chooses the ad from the app, the kind of device and your rough location (from your internet address), not from a profile of you. The app never shows the "Allow this app to track you?" prompt and does not use the advertising identifier (on Android, the app removes the permission for it).

What Google still collects. To show ads, count how often an ad was shown, measure and pay for ads, and find fraud, Google's ad software (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and its consent software (User Messaging Platform) may collect:

DataWhat it is
Coarse locationYour rough location (country or city) from your IP address. The app never asks for your exact location
Device identifiersIds that Google's software uses on this device, not the advertising identifier
Product interactionHow you use the app, for example app launches, taps and video views
Advertising dataWhich ads you saw and tapped
DiagnosticsCrash logs and performance data

Google is responsible for this data under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. We only see totals from Google (for example how many ads were shown and what they earned), not data about you.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks adults for their choice before ads load, and Me > Settings > Ad privacy choices changes it at any time. Players under 18 are not asked. You can also use Google's My Ad Center.

Young people. 13-15: non-personalised ads for general audiences, and tagged as under the age of consent. 16-17: non-personalised, ads rated for teens at most. 18 and older: non-personalised, ads rated for teens at most. We block ads for gambling, dating, get-rich-quick schemes, loans and alcohol.

5. Purchases

Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) handles every payment for Plus, "No ads", "All Journey levels", "All past puzzles" and "Tile colours". We never see your name, account, card or address. The store tells the app what you own so it can unlock it, and gives us sales reports that do not identify you. See Apple's privacy policy or the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Word reports and emails to us

  • Word reports stay on your phone. Sending them is optional: "Email my word reports" opens your own mail app with the reports in a new email, and nothing is sent until you press Send.
  • When you email us, we receive your email address, any name your email account shows and what you wrote. We use it only to answer you and to fix word lists, and delete it within 12 months.

7. Other features, all on your phone

  • Reminders are notifications your phone schedules itself. The app asks for permission only when you turn one on.
  • Apple Intelligence (on iPhones that have it) writes an example sentence for a word and a weekly league headline on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us or to a cloud AI.
  • Checking guesses and "Look it up" use the word lists in the app and your phone's built-in dictionary and spell checker.
  • Pasting scores: the app reads what you copied only when you tap Paste.
  • Analytics: none. The app sends no usage statistics to us or to an analytics company.

8. This website

emrehalamannnn.github.io shows shared scores and invites, this policy and the support page. It uses no cookies, no analytics, no external fonts and no scripts from other sites. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The part of a link after "#" (the score data) is never sent to the website.

9. Children and teens

  • Wordlands is made for people aged 13 and up.
  • A player who says they are under 13 sees no ads (Google's ad software is never started), is offered no purchases and cannot use synced leagues. Nothing leaves the phone except links they choose to share. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.
  • Teens (13-17) see only non-personalised ads with the stricter rules in section 4. Group stats are shown only to players 16 and older, and "Show my place among players" only to adults.
  • There is no chat, no public profile and no way to find strangers. A league only has people who share its code or invite link.

11. Your rights

Under the EU and UK GDPR, Türkiye's KVKK (Law No. 6698) and similar laws, you have the right to know whether we hold data about you, to get a copy, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, to take it with you, and to withdraw your consent at any time (for synced leagues: delete your synced data). Write to emrehlmn@icloud.com and we will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

Why we may use the data (legal bases). Synced leagues: your consent, and running the leagues you asked for. Stopping abuse of the server (the hashed network addresses, name reports): our legitimate interest in keeping leagues safe. Emails: our legitimate interest in answering you. Ads in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: storing and reading data on your device is based on your choice in Google's consent form.

12. Selling, sharing and where data goes

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising. Data leaves your phone only as this policy says: to our server for synced leagues (Supabase, EU), to Google for ads, to Apple or Google for purchases, and to the people you send links to. Google, Apple and GitHub may process data in the United States and other countries under their own safeguards.

13. Security

Data on your phone is protected by your phone (for example, with a passcode its files are encrypted). The connection to our server is encrypted (HTTPS), the database cannot be read with the key inside the app, and only the server's own functions can read or write it. Links are not encrypted: anyone who has a link can read it.

14. Changes

When we change this policy, we change the effective date at the top. For important changes we will also say so in the app's update notes.
